Biography

 Hu Xu is a Ph.D. candidate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His research focuses on Natural Language Processing, complex reasoning, and large language models (LLMs). He has published papers at top-tier venues including EMNLP and BMC Bioinformatics. He is an active open-source contributor, with projects such as llm-cookbook (23.5k stars) and llm-universe (12.2k stars). He has interned at Datawhale, Sinovation Ventures, and JD.com, working on LLM training and applications.

Research Interest

 I work in the field of Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models. Currently, I focus on the following research topics:
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Complex Reasoning and fundamental research of LLMs
  • Multi-Agent (Debate, Role-Playing)
I love collaborating with others! If you're interested in working together, feel free to reach out — don't hesitate!😊
