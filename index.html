About

I'm a PhD candidate at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Kun Kuang. I work at the intersection of LLM post-training, efficient inference, and multi-agent systems — with recent threads on data-quality diagnostics for reasoning, capability-balanced pruning, and communication-efficient cooperative perception.

Beyond research, I'm an active open-source contributor to the Datawhale community. My repositories llm-cookbook (24k+ ★) and llm-universe (14k+ ★) are widely used Chinese-language tutorials for LLM engineering. I also co-led the Chinese localization of Stanford's CS336 course.

Structure Trumps Size: Rethinking Data Quality for LLM Reasoning

Hu Xu, Zeyan Li, Rui Wang, Jianfeng Xu
💡 A small but CoT-correct, semantically diverse dataset can outperform an 8× larger unfiltered one across 7 reasoning benchmarks — for LLM reasoning, data structure trumps data size.

SemRD-V2X: Closure-Guided Communication with Bounded Inference for Cooperative Perception

Hu Xu, Chun Li, Siyuan Qiu, Zeyan Li, Jianfeng Xu
💡 We reformulate V2X cooperative perception under a closure-fidelity lens from source coding, and instantiate it as SemRD-V2X — a BEV-selecting + pointwise-compressing + masked-reconstruction design that cuts communication by 26.6× while gaining +3.81 mean AP on V2XSet / DAIR-V2X.
📄 PDF

TransBindpMHCI: A Transformer-based Model for Pan-specific MHC-I Peptide Binding Prediction

Hu Xu, Yuanli Ni, Zixuan Chai, Xuan Cui, Xia Lei, Limei Liu, Juanjuan Shan, Cheng Qian
💡 TransBindpMHCI is a pan-specific Transformer that jointly encodes peptide and MHC-I sequences to predict binding affinity across alleles — outperforming CNN baselines on cross-allele generalization.

Averaged Evaluation Masks Capability Trade-Offs: Multi-Source Calibration for High-Sparsity LLM Pruning

Hu Xu, Zhaolong Xing, Congcong Liu, Jiaxing Wang, Zhida Jiang, Junshi Huang, Zhen Chen, Jianfeng Xu
💡 We show that averaged evaluation hides 50+ point capability trade-offs in LLM-pruning calibration, and propose IGSP — a self-generated, capability-stratified multi-source calibration that closes 49% of the gap to real multi-source data without any pretraining-corpus access.
