About

I'm a PhD candidate at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Jianfeng Xu (许建峰). My research focuses on natural language processing, LLM post-training (SFT / RL / OPD), and LLM-based agents. I have hands-on experience with agentic-RL methods including GRPO, OPD, OPSD, GSPO, DAPO, Search-R1, ReTool, ALFWorld, Vision GRPO, and Slime.

Beyond research, I'm an active open-source contributor to the Datawhale community. My repositories llm-cookbook (21.9k+ ★) and llm-universe (10.5k+ ★) are widely used Chinese-language tutorials for LLM engineering. I also co-led the Chinese localization of Stanford's CS336 course.

LLM Pruning Calibration

Averaged Evaluation Masks Capability Trade-Offs: Multi-Source Calibration for High-Sparsity LLM Pruning

[一作] Hu Xu, Zhaolong Xing, Congcong Liu, Jiaxing Wang, Zhida Jiang, Junshi Huang, Zhen Chen, Jianfeng Xu
💡 We dissect 15 calibration sources for LLM pruning under LLaMA-3.1-8B and find that the famous 2.85-point averaged gap masks a 51.9-point Code-vs-General trade-off — IGSP (information-guided self-calibration) closes 49% of the gap to real multi-source data without any pretraining-corpus access, beating the strongest single source by 8.8 points at 60% SparseGPT sparsity.

LLM Reasoning Data Quality

Structure Trumps Size: Rethinking Data Quality for LLM Reasoning

[一作] Hu Xu, Zeyan Li, Rui Wang, Jianfeng Xu
💡 Problem: reasoning-data pipelines usually optimize for scale and surface cleanliness, while the effect of data structure is poorly understood. Method: MCSQ evaluates data along 6 orthogonal dimensions and uses controlled composition experiments. Conclusion: selectively retaining "distorted" or "mismatched" samples can outperform conventional clean data, showing that dataset structure matters more than size in this regime.

Bioinformatics MHC-I

TransBindpMHCI: A Transformer-based Model for Pan-specific MHC-I Peptide Binding Prediction

[一作] Hu Xu, Yuanli Ni, Zixuan Chai, Xuan Cui, Xia Lei, Limei Liu, Juanjuan Shan, Cheng Qian
💡 TransBindpMHCI is a pan-specific MHC-I binding predictor trained on 1.4M mass-spectrometry peptides, using a dual-tier Transformer encoder for stronger feature extraction with reduced complexity. It supports peptides of 8–15 amino acids, achieves cross-species (human + murine) prediction, and outperforms existing methods in accuracy, efficiency, and generalizability — validated on HPV-vaccine and tumor-neoantigen datasets.

General Information Metrics for Improving AI Model Training Efficiency

Jianfeng Xu, Congcong Liu, Xiaoying Tan, Xiaojie Zhu, Anpeng Wu, Huan Wan, Weijun Kong, Chun Li, Hu Xu, Kun Kuang, Fei Wu
💡 We generalize compression-style information metrics to two unified forms — OIT and MLT-MF — the only two metrics that consistently correlate with capability retention across tasks, turning "information" into a drop-in training-efficiency objective for any AI model.

V2X Cooperative Perception

SemRD-V2X: Closure-Guided Communication with Bounded Inference for Cooperative Perception

[一作] Hu Xu, Chun Li, Siyuan Qiu, Zeyan Li, Jianfeng Xu
💡 Introduces a closure-fidelity perspective answering a more fundamental question than "how to compress": which remote evidence is truly indispensable when the ego vehicle can infer the rest locally? SemRD-V2X operationalizes the derived principle (transmit compact evidence, recover derivable context) via exact-budget BEV selection + pointwise compression + masked reconstruction — cutting feature payload 26.6× while improving AP@0.5/0.7 by 4.13/8.57 points with only 3.81% extra latency.
